How to use

1. Click New Outer Bound or New Inner Bound.

2. Click on the map to place pins.

3. You can also press My Location to use your phone's GPS location as a pin.

4. Use My Location multiple times to add multiple GPS pins to the same boundary.

5. Add at least 3 points, then press Finish Current Bound.

6. Create another outer or inner bound whenever you want.

You can create as many outer and inner bounds as needed.
